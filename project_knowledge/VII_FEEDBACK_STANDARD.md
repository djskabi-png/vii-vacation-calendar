# VII accepted feedback standard

This file records accepted product behavior reconstructed from the verified 72-hour audit ending 3 November 2026. It is a requirement source, not proof that production currently implements every item.

## Search and availability

- Dated vacation results rank truthful online-bookable offers with price and availability first. Telephone-ready, price-only, unknown and unavailable states follow in that order.
- An online badge requires supported booking, a priced offer and verified availability for the selected stay.
- Places without an online calendar show a concise telephone-only state with phone and WhatsApp actions.
- Price sorting is numeric, stable and based on the price the visitor sees.
- Accommodation type filtering includes every verified primary and secondary type.
- Accessibility and feature filters use normalized supplier identities, not a truncated display-feature list.
- Desktop selection may apply immediately. Mobile keeps the staged chooser.

## Pages, SEO and navigation

- Approved type, city, region and semantic-filter intents use real ASCII canonical pages with appropriate metadata, content, FAQ, breadcrumb and sitemap membership.
- Dates, guests, price and sorting are transactional parameters and do not create indexable pages by default.
- Last-minute events, spa today, spa tomorrow and all four near-weekend periods have unique permanent pages.
- Popular searches remain in one horizontally scrollable row and link to their correct destinations.
- Legacy redirects are direct, intentional and free of chains, loops or generic-home fallbacks.

## Place pages and media

- General gallery opens first. Unit galleries keep the correct unit identity and sequence.
- Broken media may use an explicitly valid fallback but may not substitute another unit or business.
- All verified rooms and bedroom facts appear. Area is shown when supplied.
- Amenities and services appear before the sleeping section.
- Long text shows an expand control only when content actually overflows.
- Per-place cancellation terms appear in the booking flow when supplied.
- Supplier videos appear only when a verified upstream video contract exists.

## Booking and contact

- Spa booking stays inside VII.
- WhatsApp opens with the supplier WhatsApp number and does not depend on optional lead persistence.
- A visible booking promise must have a working booking action. Otherwise the UI presents the truthful contact path.

## Performance and interaction

- Navigation must never leave a visitor on a blank page or contradictory stale/error state.
- Sliders preserve the last verified result during a bounded refresh and never show an error and usable results simultaneously.
- Maps load progressively, preserve search state and remain usable on desktop, mobile and landscape transitions.
- Responsive images use the best available supplier source without inventing detail above the original.

## Administration and release

- World shutdown controls affect the public world.
- Professor answers use connected data, readable RTL layout and isolated English, numeric, date and currency runs.
- QQQAAA includes the complete administration control inventory, accessibility and typography contracts.
- Every candidate must pass the protected release-source gate before publication.
- A public claim requires exact deployed identity and natural-destination verification.

The detailed status of each accepted item is maintained in the 72-hour closure ledger referenced by the master plan.

## Event availability and fixed-route state, 4 November 2026

- Event inventory and online price are separate facts. Positive supplier freeUnits retains an unpriced venue. Null or absent inventory stays unknown. An unverified roomID or occupied advertising-floor price must not become an offer.
- Event result completeness must survive every layer: provider normalization, both-slot snapshot, public-card join and UI. An editorial20-card limit is not an inventory limit. Compare identities as well as counts.
- Each event preset owns its permanent page and query date. A fixed route preset overrides retained client selection immediately, including forward/back navigation and the14:00 cutoff. A useState initializer is not synchronization with a changed route prop.
- Retain only correctly scoped, age-valid snapshots; disclose stale availability, and require final confirmation before booking. Verify stale-to-fresh recovery at the public destination.
- Preserve the v796 event repair and its retained-client navigation regression tests in cumulative releases. Public event repair evidence does not close the separate platform performance, memory or scheduler findings.

## Browser proximity and spa weekday pages, 4 November 2026

- Every spa weekday intent has a distinct permanent ASCII page and slider destination, with metadata, localized content, FAQ and sitemap membership. Its next occurrence follows the Israel-time discovery cutoff and must update while the page remains open.
- A full weekday page is not capped to a slider's20 offers. It requests only the selected date rather than eagerly loading every day's full inventory.
- Every result world exposes nearest-first sorting. Choosing browser location in search selects that order and ranks by the retained browser point, rather than replacing it with a city filter and recommended ordering.
- Known positions sort by straight-line distance; missing coordinates remain visible after known positions. Regional trail estimates are disclosed as regional, never precise trailhead distances.
- Location denial and timeout have an explicit retry state. Coordinates never enter shareable URLs or analytics. Mobile keeps its staged search and manual submit; desktop retains immediate location selection behavior.
- These are accepted requirements. Their presence here does not prove a public release or live verification.
